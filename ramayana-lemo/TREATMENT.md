The voiceover (4:51) is the clock. Every scene and every line of text is placed by the Whisper-aligned timeline (timeline.json).

## Brief changes from Part 1 v1
- Rebuilt on the Lemo-Opuscar Gilded Illumination kit: flat mineral pigments inside ink outlines, white-lead dots, gold leaf laid in squares that is dark until a light inside the picture crosses it (glint pass per leaf square), deep ultramarine night (never black), a vellum speech scroll for subtitles.
- **One tempo for all motion** (requested by the channel): every shot pushes in at the same rate (log-zoom 0.0055/s, drift 5 px/s), all writing is pen-wiped at one speed (520 px/s; shlokas at 400 px/s, close to the chant), every element builds in 1.0 s, every scene change is the same cloud-bank wipe (1.6 s, linear), a band of light crosses the gold every 9 s in 3.2 s. (Lemo's director guide asks for varied pace; the channel's request wins.)
- Background score added (original, synthesized), ducked under the voice.
- No page turns and no cross-dissolves (the style forbids them): one transition, the cloud bank, used everywhere.

## Logline and arc
A sage asks whether one complete human being exists; the answer, carried by a veena from heaven, is a name lettered in gold.
Setup (the question in lamplight) → the ashram and the asking (shlokas 1.1.1–1.1.5) → the sixteen qualities lit as one lotus → Narada's answer (1.1.7–1.1.8) → the touch on राम → Ayodhya rejoices, then its lamps go out → the whole picture, the next part.

## Score
Bhupali on D (Sa Re Ga Pa Dha) over a tanpura drone; a veena-like plucked string with meend and gamak; harp for chords; temple bells and hand bells when gold lights; a frame drum for Ayodhya. The band stops at the turn (tanpura only), a low bell, then komal Ga and Ni. The end card resolves the opening phrase on Sa. Music ≈ 20 dB under the voice while he speaks, rising in pauses; −14 LUFS master.
